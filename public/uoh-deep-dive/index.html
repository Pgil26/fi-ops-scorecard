Findings

Older US accounts — start years 2013 through 2019 — average $50.07 per unit. Accounts that started in 2020–2023 average $53.64. Starts from 2024 through 2026 drop to $51.54. The newest US book is still above the oldest cohort, and below the 2020–2023 peak. Those three figures are unit-weighted averages from the start-year summary.

Small units follow the “newer is pricier” pattern. On the US book, small-unit prices sit in the mid-$30s through the middle of the last decade and in the mid-$40s for recent starts (2025 is $46.92, 2026 is $46.05). Large units on recent starts are cheaper. After a 2023 peak of $68.95, large-unit prices are $54.76 in 2024, $52.86 in 2025, and $54.86 in 2026.

CAD shows no clear trend. The all-sizes average moves between about C$53 and C$61 depending on the year, without the US step up and the later drop. The same era cuts land at C$56.05 (2015–2019), C$55.76 (2020–2023), and C$54.54 (2024–2026).

Notes and method

What a unit is
Units are active line items on Closed Won opportunities in Salesforce: the sum of line quantity where product status is Active and the opportunity stage is Closed Won. This cut is 7,457 units on 7,380 active line items.
Tenure
Tenure is measured from the earliest active line’s product start date to 2 November 2026. If that date is missing, the earliest Closed Won close date is used. Months are days divided by 30.4375. It is the age of the oldest line still active, so it can understate the relationship when earlier lines were replaced.
Start dates that look reset
Start dates appear reset for North Boston, Fort Lauderdale, San Antonio, and Vancouver. Older accounts in those cities are pushed into recent years, and the very short tenures (North Boston 1.4 months, Fort Lauderdale 11.8, San Antonio 19.1, Vancouver 23.9) should be read with that in mind. Vancouver is CAD, so it is marked on the location table and not on the USD cross grid.
Price
Monthly price is the line total (unit price times quantity), treated as the monthly hire rate. Average per account is the location monthly total divided by active accounts. Average price per unit, including by size, is quantity-weighted: total price divided by total quantity.
Currency
Toronto and Vancouver are Canadian dollars. Every other location in this cut is US dollars. Currencies are not converted. Do not add a USD dollar total to a CAD dollar total.
Scope
Pulled 2 November 2026, summary tables only. Tampa and Sao Paulo are excluded. This page does not list accounts or customers. A handful of active lines have a zero or blank price, or a quantity of zero or less; they stay in the counts and the averages.
